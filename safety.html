Firearm Safety

Welcome to the safety section of Iron and Insight. This page provides educational information about firearm safety, responsible ownership and the importance of protecting lives.

Introduction to Firearm Safety

Firearm safety refers to the rules, precautions and responsible practices that help prevent accidents, injuries and loss of lives involving firearms.

Basic Firearm Safety Principles

  • Always treat every firearm as potentially dangerous.
  • Never point a firearm at another person.
  • Keep your finger away from the trigger unless lawfully and properly prepared to use it.
  • Never handle a firearm under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Keep firearms away from children and unauthorized persons.
  • Seek qualified safety education before handling a firearm.

Safe Storage of Firearms

Proper storage helps prevent unauthorized access, accidents and misuse.

  • Store firearms in a securely locked safe.
  • Keep ammunition locked separately.
  • Restrict access to authorized adults only.
  • Never leave firearms unattended.

Responsible Ownership

Responsible firearm ownership involves obeying applicable laws, obtaining the necessary legal authorization, understanding safety requirements and ensuring that firearms do not endanger other people.

Firearm Safety and Children

Children should never be allowed to handle firearms. Parents, teachers and guardians should educate children about the dangers of firearms and teach them to stay away and immediately inform a trusted adult if they discover one.

What to Do When You Find a Firearm

  1. Do not touch or pick it up.
  2. Move away from the area.
  3. Keep other people away.
  4. Inform a responsible adult or the appropriate authorities.

Conclusion

Firearm safety is everyone's responsibility. Education, proper storage, legal compliance and responsible behaviour help protect individuals, families and communities.
